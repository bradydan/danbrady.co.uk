About

Dan Brady is a documentary photographer based near Newcastle-upon-Tyne. He works on gatherings — festivals, night shifts, crossings, union halls — and photographs the hours that surround them rather than the event itself.

He studied at Northumbria University in the 1990s and worked for years as a freelance photographer before turning to long-form projects — each shot over weeks in one place and edited down to a sequence of no more than thirty-two frames. Assignments have taken him to Oaxaca, Rotterdam, Reykjavík and the eastern Aegean.
